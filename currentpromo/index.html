Got Questions

Quick Answers

Is there really no minimum price I have to pay for building the website?
Yes, you can pay as low as 0$ for building your site, but you must host with us and pick one of our monthly maintainance plans. This offer exists because we believe every Canadian business deserves a professional online presence, regardless of budget. You won't be judged for naming a low number.
What counts as an "outdated" website?
Generally: not mobile-friendly, more than 3 years without a refresh, broken pages, missing SSL, or a site that no longer accurately reflects what you offer. If you're embarrassed to hand someone your URL, it qualifies.
What if I need features beyond what Silver covers?
No problem, we quote those separately. The site built under this offer covers a professional, fast, multi-page business website. If you later need a new page, booking form, or redesign, we'll scope it and price it clearly with no surprises.
How long does the offer last?
This is a limited-availability promotion with no fixed end date — but we only take on a set number of Pick Your Own Price clients at a time to ensure quality. Once spots are filled, the offer closes. Reaching out today is the best move.
Do I own my website?
Yes. The content, copy, and design are yours. While we host and maintain the site under Silver, you retain full ownership. If you ever move on, we'll help you transition your content — no hostage-taking here.
Can I cancel after the 3-month minimum?
Absolutely. After the initial 3-month commitment, Silver is fully month-to-month. Give us 30 days notice and you're free to go, no penalties, no drama.
